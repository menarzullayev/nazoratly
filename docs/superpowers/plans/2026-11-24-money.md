# G1 money rules (session S1, slug `money`, migrations 0020-0024)

Scope: design `2026-11-24-product-expansion-2-design.md` section 4 G1, in BOTH products, identical rules proven by `tests/golden/money.json` (+ new cases in `accounting.json`).

1. Refunds, four kinds, all through the journal.
2. Four lasting discounts (family, individual, scholarship, early payment) in charge generation.
3. No late-payment penalty (reminders only): proven by negative tests.
4. Daily cash close (smena) linked to the journal; the monthly period close stays.

## Decisions (made here, within the owner's wording)

Money is whole so'm; all rounding is half up to a whole so'm.

### Discounts
- Order and cap: base = fee after the legacy `enrollment.discount_percent`; lasting discounts apply in the order scholarship, family, individual, each computed on the BASE (percent) or fixed (amount); the sum is capped so the net never goes below 0 (later ones are truncated).
- family: center settings `family_discount_2nd_percent`, `family_discount_3rd_percent` (0 = off). Children of one family = students sharing a guardian (API: a shared `student_guardians` guardian; Sheets: same guardian name). Billable children of the month ordered by student id (registration order): 1st 0 %, 2nd p2, 3rd and later p3.
- individual / scholarship: per-student rules (`student_discounts`) with percent OR amount (scholarship percent only, 100 = free), a reason (>= 3 chars), validity dates; optional enrollment scope (without it: percent applies to every tuition charge, amount applies per charge).
- early payment: per-student rule with percent and `pay_by_day` (1-28). The charge keeps its full (post lasting discount) price; `early_discount` and `early_pay_by` are stored; the discount is GRANTED (charge amount reduced, discount line written) when allocations of money paid on or before `early_pay_by` cover `amount - early_discount`. Money allocated beyond the reduced price returns to the student's credit. Charges with a payment plan never get an early discount.
- A fully free month (net 0) creates no charge (amount > 0 constraint) but its discount lines are kept (`charge_id` NULL) so the scholarship report still shows it.
- Discounts never touch the journal (cash basis: tuition income is booked when money is received). Report `GET /v1/reports/discounts?month=` totals by kind, scholarship separate.

### Refunds
Kinds: `wrong_payment` (money back for a mistaken payment), `leaving` (proportional remainder of a month), `package_remainder` (value of unused package lessons), `to_credit` (paid money kept as the student's credit, no cash).
- payout: `cash` | `bank` (journal: Dr 4200 Qaytarilgan to'lovlar, Cr 1000/1010) or `credit` (no money moves, no journal entry: nothing to post). `to_credit` is always `credit`.
- leaving: used = round(amount * left_day / days_in_month) (the leave day counts as attended; left before the month = 0, after = all); reduction = amount - used; money = max(paid - used, 0). The charge is reduced to `used` (voided when 0).
- package_remainder: money = min(lessons_money(remaining lessons), paid on the package charge); the charge is reduced by the money.
- to_credit: charge + amount (<= paid on that charge); the charge is reduced by amount.
- API: allocations are released newest first; cash refunds are split over payments (`refund_payments`, `payments.refunded`); a payment with refunds cannot be voided.
- Sheets: no allocations: sheet `Qaytarishlar`; `computeDebts` subtracts cash payouts from the paid pool and `chargeReduction` from the charge. Journal entries via `syncLedger` (source type `refund`).
- Chart gets 4200 Qaytarilgan to'lovlar (income, contra: debit side) and 5900 Kassa farqi (expense); `ensure_chart` seeds missing codes for existing centers.

### Cash close
`cash_closes`: date, shift_no, counted, book_balance (journal cash account 1000 through the date), difference = counted - book, note (required when difference != 0), journal head seq at close time, optional adjusting entry (`post_difference`: over -> Dr 1000 / Cr 4100, short -> Dr 5900 / Cr 1000, source type `cash_close`). Immutable; no void (a mistaken close is answered by the next close).
