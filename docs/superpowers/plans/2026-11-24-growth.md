# G3 Growth (session S3, slug `growth`, migrations 0030-0034)

Scope = design section 4 / G3, both products. Charge generation is never edited (S1 owns it): every reward or promo
discount is booked as an open **credit** in the existing credit mechanism (`absence_entitlements`, kind `credit`;
Sheets sheet "Qoplash huquqlari"), which `finance.generate` / Sheets billing already consume. Nothing here sends a
message to a lead, student or guardian (Model B); reminders are an internal list.

## Decisions (owner already decided the product behavior; these are builder choices)

- Credit table generalised in 0030: `source` (`absence`/`referral`/`promo`), nullable enrollment/group/lesson for non-absence credits, `reference` text.
- A credit is consumed whole by generation (excess lost). A reward is therefore split into chunks no larger than the referrer's monthly fee (`split_credit`) so a big fixed reward is spread over months instead of being lost.
- Referral reward is paid to the referrer when the condition holds for the invited student; booking is idempotent (one reward per referral) and runs from an explicit action (API `POST /v1/referrals/book`, Sheets menu), not from the payment code path (that belongs to S1).
- `free_lesson` reward = N x lesson value (same `lesson_credit_amount` rule as excused absences).
- Cohort definition for ROI: leads created in the range; revenue = live payments of their students up to the range end; cost = entered costs of the months touched by the range.
- Reminders: due follow-up date, silent N days (default 3), after a trial. A planned future follow-up silences the reminder.
